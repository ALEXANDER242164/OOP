# Value Proposition
## What makes the system different?
The system was created to make access to psychological care easy and organized for both patients and administrative staff.
How does it help?

It allows anyone interested in receiving psychological support to register and request appointments quickly. However, the patient does not choose the date, time, or therapist directly. Instead, a secretary or coordinator manages the calendar in a centralized way.

This ensures personalized care, avoids schedule conflicts, and keeps the process professionally controlled. Once the secretary assigns the appointment, the patient receives a notification with the confirmed details (day, time, therapist, and room).
Also, if the patient needs to change the appointment, they can send a rescheduling request, which will also be reviewed and managed by the secretary.

Unlike other platforms that allow free scheduling, our system focuses on professional management of psychological appointments, maintaining constant and organized communication between the patient and the administrative staff.

#### The system offers:

- User/patient registration
- Appointment and rescheduling requests
- Appointment assignment and management by the secretary
- Automatic notifications to the patient about confirmations or changes
- Access to appointment history

This way, we provide a complete tool that makes the process of requesting and receiving psychological care safe, reliable, and professionally coordinated.
